The preceding RC7 source passed all main platform checks and all seven GNOME release cases, but
publication stopped because its final validator lacked `fflate`. RC5, RC6 and RC7 were not
published; their tags remain unchanged. These notes cover the cumulative changes since the last
published RC4. RC8 publication requires its own fresh platform/native gates, rebuilt assets and
complete validation.

Representative full local GNOME 42 controls passed with and without tracing, and a genuine 80-pixel
mismatch was rejected by the original assertion. Tracing can change scheduling, and finite passes do
not establish a geometry cure. Earlier GNOME 42/48 bounds mismatches and GNOME 50 original
concurrent-load/cycle31 failures remain unresolved. Physical GPU/display/device configurations and
higher GNOME 50 native resource scales remain outside software qualification.
